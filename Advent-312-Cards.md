# Peakopoly — Advent Anarchy: 312-card catalogue

13 players × 24 days. The server shuffles 286 main cards for days 1–22 and 26 closing cards for days 23–24; every card is dealt once.

Cards unlock at London midnight from 1 December. Missed cards wait until the finale on 24 December at 19:00, and open oldest first before normal gameplay. Pending Black Friday envelopes resolve first. No cash balance goes below zero.

Effects use holdings and net worth at resolution. Asset transfers cancel affected pending offers, carry existing upgrades to the recipient, and can break sets; upgrades removed by confiscation or sale are not refunded unless the card says so. Complete sets are still required for further upgrades. Assets are valued at original purchase price plus upgrade investment when selecting highest/lowest.

Conditional effects have fallbacks. Tax percentages round down. Cash payments are limited to available funds. Collection cards choose eligibility once at the start of the effect. Stolen/swapped assets target richer players or larger portfolios, as written. Random selection and gambles use server randomness.

Bonus rolls last seven days from resolution, capped by the finale, and sit outside normal storage. Bank cover adds to existing cover, applies to the next N actual board rolls and ends at the finale. Normal daily rolls and the normal storage cap are unchanged.

From 23 December, unresolved disruptive main cards pay £50 instead. Positive grants, non-negative cash/roll bundles, bank cover and unowned-asset claims still resolve normally. The separate closing deck cannot confiscate holdings. There is one full-liquidation card.

Exactly 13 active non-observer accounts are required at the draw. Later signups do not join this fixed deck. An inactive player keeps their allocations; they are not redealt. All cards expire at the finale.

Admin desk → Advent card laboratory simulates a chosen card on a cloned player state, without changing holdings, feed or actual assignments. Catalogue disclosure does not reveal who was dealt which cards.
